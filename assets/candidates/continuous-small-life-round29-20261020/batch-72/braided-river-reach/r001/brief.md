# Frozen premodel brief

Original generic shallow braided alluvial river reach, a static process analogue. Not a Snake River replica, named-site survey, precise age, hydrologic simulation or ecosystem reconstruction.

- connected shallow inset multithread water network
- multiple genuine splitting and rejoining nodes
- several unequal tapered natural exposed sediment bars
- continuous physical floodplain with channel banks below dry bar tops, no decorative islands on one meander

Long-axis Y flow; branch network repeatedly splits and rejoins around three offset bar groups and a cross-connecting arm. Water surface kept below continuous bank terrain and unequal exposed bar crests; terrain is carved by distance to the same branch-network centerlines. Bar forms arise in network voids rather than separate discs placed on a water sheet. Closed terrain cutaway with natural low relief and a few embedded faceted gravel stones. No vegetation required for bare sediment reference scope. Concept inspected: connectivity useful, but concept has overly repeated symmetric almond bars. Correct final branch network to unequal asymmetric widths, staggered junctions and differently shaped bars, not copy those ovals.
