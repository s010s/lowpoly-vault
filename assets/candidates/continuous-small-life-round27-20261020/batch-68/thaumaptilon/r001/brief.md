# Frozen premodel brief

Evidence-informed whole-organism outline reconstruction of Thaumaptilon walcotti, not a modern sea pen or exact specimen replica. Nominal212mm height references ROM complete holotype USNM468028; width50mm follows reported maximum, thickness/color/pose are artistic. Morphological affinity remains disputed: ROM describes historical stem-cnidarian interpretation, Hoyal Cuthill2022 supports Petalonamae. Neither is asserted as settled. Basal anchoring interpretation and pustules-as-zooids uncertain; no feeding tentacles invented.

- Complete narrow flattened oblong frond around a continuous central axis, gentle taper to tip and blunt basal attachment
- About three dozen angled lateral ridges/structures joined through common basal frond rather than independent comb branches or fern leaflets
- Subtle irregular pustules on one side only; no unpreserved tentacles or settled cnidarian/modern sea-pen classification

Actual concept has correct connected narrow lamina and short blunt base, but is overly symmetrical and overregular. Retain continuous full-volume frond, subtly unequal lateral structures and low relief on both broad surfaces, sparse haphazard pustules on front only. Eighteen oblique structures each side as approximate about-three-dozen layout, not fossil-count assertion. No open negative spaces between structures: connected common sheet. Short basal attachment remains partly frond-like and blunt, not a big modern sea-pen bulb or plant roots. Rear has angled relief but no pustules. Thin solid cross-section, gentle bent pose, no rock slab or environment base.
