# Frozen premodel brief

Original stylized aragonite-frostwork habit concept, not an assayed mineral specimen or named cave replica. Acicular form and radiating growth are supported; chosen host layout,5bundles, opaque ivory palette and20cm teaching scale are artistic. No claim every natural frostwork is aragonite.

- Five asymmetric airy radiating bundles of slender straight pointed acicular needles
- Secondary finer needles emerge from primary stems with clear air gaps, not blunt quartz towers or curled gypsum petals
- Small uneven limestone host with minor nodular growth; crystal crust dominates without pedestal

Concept D aesthetic clusters retained but its thick blunt-ended square rods rejected: actual straight needles become slender closed5-sided tapering prisms with sharp endpoints, branching secondaries narrower still. Five nodular source locations irregularly positioned on a compact limestone host. Around16primary needles per bundle plus finer sprigs along some stems, not broad crystals or flowers. Opaque rough ivory/cool cream PBR for portability, no transparency/refraction fidelity claim. All needles volumetric with positive radii and capped ends; component intersections at growth origins intentional, no floating stems. Host receives2quiet warmgray materials, needles2ivory materials.
