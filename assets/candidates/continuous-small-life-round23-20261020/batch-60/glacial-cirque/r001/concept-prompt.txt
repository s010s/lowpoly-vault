Original low-poly sculpture concept design sheet for FOUR distinct natural-history teaching assets. White background, restrained matte palette, deliberate faceting and full volumetric forms. No labels inside objects, no museum stands, no frames, no texture photographs. Four clearly separate labeled horizontal rows A-D. Each row has a large three-quarter hero at left and compatible small side and top views at right. All views of a row depict the same form. Do not copy any existing illustration.
A COLLINSIUM: a complete slender Cambrian lobopodian, roughly85mm body length. Head at left, blunt ventrally curved eyeless head, tiny forward antenna pair and paired tiny head plates. Elongated cylindrical gently arching russet body. Exactly15 paired limb stations: the first SIX pairs elongated fine anterior filtering arms reach downward and slightly forward with comb-like paired fine bristles along their inner sides; the final NINE pairs are stout short walking lobopods with backward-recurved terminal claws. Posterior short tapering trunk extension. At the first station only two tiny dorsolateral spines; subsequent14 stations each have five spines: one tall central dorsal, two shorter dorsolateral and two small lateral spines. Central spines graduate taller at middle then shorter at rear, elegant separated pointed silhouette, not a generic centipede, no arthropod armor plates and no eyes. Rust/brown soft body and darker ochre spine tips. Show complete front-to-tail, no crop; documentary stylized reconstruction not cartoon monster.
B YARDANG: single elongated wind-eroded compact sediment/rock ridge, not a loose-sand dune. Long axis recedes from blunt tall rounded windward nose at left/front to low narrow tapering lee tail at right/rear. Steep eroded flanks with longitudinal grooves and a few horizontal exposed rock ledges, broad asymmetric faceted crest, pale ochre/earth strata. No crescent horns, no sand slipface, no symmetric dune, no large scenic base. Approximately8m long,2.8m wide,2.5m tall.
C GLACIAL CIRQUE: mountain head hollow as a compact terrain cutaway volume. A steep horseshoe amphitheatre headwall enclosing a genuinely excavated deep basin floor; high jagged rear rim and lowering side shoulders, front open to short valley outlet crossing a low rock threshold. No complete circular bowl wall and no crater. Bare gray slate rock, a few restrained residual snow patches high in shaded headwall gullies. A small optional opaque pale blue tarn low in the basin, not filling it to rim. Clearly legible hollow and headwall in hero and top, rocky sloping outside/back and closed cutaway underside. Nominal80m wide90m deep35m high, teaching module not named site.
D ARAGONITE FROSTWORK: small limestone fragment encrusted by three to five airy sprays of straight slender white/ivory needle crystals. Clusters radiate from small irregular nodules, secondary finer straight needles branching from radiating stems, open negative spaces. Sharp narrow acicular crystals, not broad flat petals, not curled gypsum flowers, not snow-covered cactus and not chunky quartz hexagonal towers. A delicate spiny crust growing from a small uneven warm gray limestone host, tiny scale20cm across. Fine crystal tips have slight cool ivory tone, opaque nonmetallic. Separate radiating bundles, asymmetry and readable branching.
