# Frozen premodel brief

Original generic geological-form teaching module, not a site reconstruction, glacial simulation or measured mountain. No age/locality or simultaneous ecosystem claim. Nominal80x90m footprint and36m relief are artistically scaled for scene use.

- Deep excavated amphitheatre hollow with steep high rear headwall and lowering lateral shoulders
- Basin floor below headwall connected through low front threshold to open valley outlet, not closed crater
- Rocky enclosing terrain volume with restrained residual snow in high gullies, full closed cutaway underside

Concept C retains headwall, basin, jagged rim and muted gray exposed rock. Reject its excessive circular enclosure: deliberately lower and open front-Y throat leading over a low threshold into short valley outlet. High rear+Y wall wraps side shoulders into U-shaped amphitheatre, outer/back slopes exist in3D. Continuous faceted terrain with closed cutaway underside. Small snow patches on upper interior wall only, not an active-glacier depiction. Omit optional tarn to keep actual basin relief readable in top/clay and avoid waterplane ambiguity. Facet color subordinate to geometry, 3gray rocks+snow.
