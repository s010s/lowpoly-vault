# Hypoturrilites turret ammonite — frozen r001 concept brief

Original low-poly direction: establish tall tight helicoid shell with rows of rounded nodes on descending whorls in the blockout, then preserve the largest silhouette breaks and openings. Simplify fine ornament and repeated elements without copying reference meshes; palette and presentation are artistic choices, not fossil evidence.

Two views show tapered cream torticone, roughly six descending whorls, spiral rows of blunt nodes. Lower mouth in hero, closed rear. Secondary rear is smaller and omits aperture; not measurable orthographic agreement. Author one continuous expanding tube with terminal bounded cavity, multiple rows of rounded nodes and coherent rear coils. No soft tissue. Reduce fine speckle and triangular noise to quiet faceted limestone regions.

Nominal Blender XYZ meters: [0.1, 0.1, 0.18]. Measured XYZ bounds are retained separately. Blender Z-up / -Y-forward, GLB Y-up / +Z-forward. Origin at floor-contact center. Child-friendly stylized teaching prop, palette only.

Continuous rear coil and nodes; lower inner aperture closes at finite depth; original shape, not a copy of PRI specimen.

Target: static gallery medium-distance and rotatable close inspection. 14,000 triangles, 6 materials, 30 primitives and 1,000,000 bytes are hard ceilings. No texture.
