# Frozen original modeling brief

Evidence-informed genus-level exterior living reconstruction, not a measured specimen or internal anatomy model. Leafless digitate branch architecture and abscission stubs follow cited fossils; color, exact spray count/pose, hidden surfaces and 2.7m illustrative scale are artistic.

Primary / secondary / tertiary structure:
- Slender upright trunk with expanded basal foot, narrowed middle and slightly broader domed apex
- Upper crown of digitate leafless branch sprays with visible air gaps
- Short retained branch bases in approximate longitudinal ranks below crown

Original sheet panel A supplies faceting, airy crown and warm trunk, but its thick repeatedly divided modern-tree boughs are NOT anatomical authority. Replace with slender lateral digitate branch sprays inserted along upper trunk, which continues unbranched to a domed apex. Approximate 6 vertical insertion ranks, 3 uneven levels, each primary branch divides distally into 3-5 finger-like axes carrying terminal dichotomous twigs. Narrow lower trunk, flared basal foot with slender non-branching roots, not oak buttresses. 18 sprays artistic simplification; no leaves/fronds/sporangia modeled, no exact crown density or internal hollow-trunk claim.
