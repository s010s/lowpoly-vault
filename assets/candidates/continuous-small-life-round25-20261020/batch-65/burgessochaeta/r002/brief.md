# Frozen premodel design

Evidence-informed whole-organism genus-level living exterior reconstruction of Burgessochaeta, not a specimen replica or museum-grade reconstruction. All body, head, paired smooth palps and trunk appendages included. Illustrative 50mm trunk, palette, pose, unseen cross-sections and bristle simplification are artistic.

- Whole slender soft segmented worm, no shell plates or dorsal protective thatch
- Two smooth anterior palps on differentiated head; no unsupported jaws, eyes or long tail filament
- 25 paired parapodial stations with BOTH roughly equal dorsal and ventral rami on each side including first trunk segment; two distinct lateral bristle fans per side/station

Concept A sets warm narrow tubular silhouette and pale fans. Reject its oversized collar-like body rings and small scattered dorsal-looking fans: use shallow real segment constrictions on continuous soft body, 25 paired stations with equant upper/lower lateral lobes and fan bristles on all stations. Head at -Y with exactly two smooth forward palps; posterior +Y short taper, no tail filament. Fan 4 tapered volumetric bristles per ramus with tiny bifid tips represented by paired terminal twigs; counts are visual simplification, not exact fossil chaetal count. Gently S-curved body, modest flattening, no substrate. Fine individual bristles readable mainly top/hero, canonical axial views necessarily foreshorten slender animal. Entire organism persists in full volume.
