- Coherent irregular matrix outcrop with embedded sharp angular clasts of multiple sizes
- Visible contrasting angular clasts across front, side, rear and top, not loose rounded pebbles
- Rough stepped asymmetrical volumetric rock mass, closed underside without pedestal
