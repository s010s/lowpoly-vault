This stylized image-to-3D-derived model has local closure, face winding and
nonmetallic material adjustments. Geometry, embedded texture and UVs elsewhere
are preserved. Colours and proportions are artistic, not specimen measurements.
The original generation service and parameters are not identified in this package.

source.blend is an editable import of this exact model.glb, with packed textures.
It is not the original author's project or the original generation project.
preview.png depicts this version. Attribution and modification notice should be
retained when sharing under CC BY 4.0.
