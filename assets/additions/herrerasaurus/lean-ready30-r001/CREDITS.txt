Herrerasaurus ischigualastensis has a deep hip region, strong grasping hands, relatively compact neck and elongate toothed skull. Skin, colour and soft tissues are inferred from a mounted skeleton that may contain restored parts, not calibrated individual anatomy.
埃雷拉龙 Herrerasaurus ischigualastensis 具有厚实髋部、强壮抓握手、相对紧凑的颈部和带齿长头骨。皮肤、颜色与软组织参照可能含修补部分的装架骨架推断，并非经校准的个体解剖复原。
