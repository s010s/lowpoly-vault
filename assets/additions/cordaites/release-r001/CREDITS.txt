Interpretive juvenile cordaitalean tree with an upper woody crown and broad strap leaves with short blunt tips and subdued parallel bands. The chosen blunt form is not a genus-wide rule. Juvenile size, 105 leaves, five visual bands, seven limbs, placement and palette are artistic; no exact fossil tree, reproductive organs or measured canopy is claimed. The 2005 ecological source was consulted as an abstract, not a full-paper verification.
解释性幼龄科达类木本植物，呈现上部木质树冠、短钝端宽带状叶及轻微平行条带。所选钝端形态并非全属统一规则；幼龄尺度、105片叶、五条视觉带、七主枝、位置与配色均属美术选择，不声称精确化石树、繁殖器官或实测树冠。2005年生态来源仅核阅摘要，并非全文核验。
