Hypothetical whole-animal Megarachne servinei, a non-marine aquatic eurypterid, not a giant spider. Fossils constrain the anterior shield, enlarged first two tergites and partial limbs; posterior body, telson, limb endings, depth and pose are inferred. Colour and the roughly 0.54 m body-length target are reconstruction choices, not complete-specimen measurements.
Megarachne servinei 的假说性全身复原，属非海相水生广翅鲎，并非巨型蜘蛛。化石约束前部背甲、增大的前两节背板及部分附肢；后躯、尾剑、足端、体厚与姿势为推测。颜色及约0.54米体长目标属复原选择，非完整标本实测。
