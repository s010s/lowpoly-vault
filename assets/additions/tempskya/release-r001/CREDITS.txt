Original Tempskya-inspired cut composite false-trunk specimen only: unequal rounded/lobed small stem islands are embedded in a dense root matrix, with interwoven cable-like root bands across the cut and outer side. Filled stem sections are larger than root strands, not concentric ordinary wood rings or punched holes. Nominal38cm, organ counts, cut relief and color are artistic; no living crown, leaves or whole fern inferred.
