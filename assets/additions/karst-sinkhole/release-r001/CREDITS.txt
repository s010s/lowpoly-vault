Original stylized karst sinkhole teaching cutaway: an enclosed surface funnel narrows into a real open throat and an enlarged underground cavity. The artificial front subsurface section reveals rock thickness and the connection without opening the surface rim. Nominal 6 m tile, dimensions, low cover, palette and exposed section are artistic choices, not measured site, dated event, collapse sequence or hydrology. No water. The artificial front cutaway and rear-half base are not collision-ready natural cave geometry. At small hero size the throat is not fully legible; top/front views are needed to explain the internal connection.
