Interpretive genus-level living plant with compact opposite-row lateral systems, small slender cones and subdued basal rhizomorph beginnings. Counts, crown outline, four basal beginnings, palette and nominal dimensions are artistic, not universal fossil facts or a specimen cast. Prior source review used relevant 2020 text/captions, not figure-pixel verification; vascular anatomy alone does not establish the external crown or roots.
属级解释性生活植物复原：紧凑对列侧枝、小型细长球果及低调的基部根状体起始。数量、冠形、四处基部起始、配色和名义尺寸属美术选择，不是普遍化石事实或标本翻模。既有来源审阅依据2020年相关正文和图注，未核验图像像素；维管解剖本身不能证明外部冠形或根形。
