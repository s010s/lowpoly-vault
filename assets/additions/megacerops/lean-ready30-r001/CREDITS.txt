Megacerops coloradensis has two unbranched bony nasal processes, with form principally guided by the AMNH 518 skeleton and skull. Species-level systematics remain debated; skin, horn covering, colour and dimensions are inferred, not exact specimen measurements.
Megacerops coloradensis 具有两枚各自不分叉的骨性鼻突，形态主要参照 AMNH 518 骨架与头骨。种级分类仍有讨论；皮肤、角表覆盖物、颜色及尺寸为推断，而非精确标本测量。
