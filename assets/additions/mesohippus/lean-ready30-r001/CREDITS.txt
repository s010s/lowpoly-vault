Mesohippus bairdi has a shorter head than modern horse proportions and three toes on each foot, including a larger central hoof. Hair, ears, posture and dimensions are interpretive; museum mounts and skull replicas do not preserve a complete living outline.
Mesohippus bairdi 的头部相对现代马比例较短，每足三趾，其中中央蹄较大。毛发、耳部、姿态及尺寸为解释性复原；博物馆装架与头骨复制品未保存完整活体轮廓。
