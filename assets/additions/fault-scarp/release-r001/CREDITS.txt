Original stylized normal-fault teaching crop: one continuous scarp separates matching high and low ground, corresponding displaced sediment bands and a shallow channel. A rounded shoulder and real cut-face bedding relief remain visible in clay. Nominal 8 m width, 1.1 m model offset, palette, crop and weathering are artistic choices, not measured site or earthquake data.
原创风格化正断层教学裁片：一处连续断层崖连接高低地面，对应沉积层和浅槽发生错移；灰模可见圆化崖肩与实体层理浅凹。名义8米宽、模型1.1米高差、配色、裁面及风化均为美术选择，不是实测地点或地震数据。
