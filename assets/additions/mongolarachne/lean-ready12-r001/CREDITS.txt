Adult female Mongolarachne jurassica reconstructed from the compressed holotype and revised interpretation. Body depth, colour, eyes, posture, missing leg tips and short palps are inferred; fine plumose setae are simplified. Relationships and web architecture remain uncertain, without implying modern Nephila identity or golden silk.
