Interpretive genus-level living tree fern with a continuous trunk, tapered root mantle and divided fronds. This bounded revision repairs root-to-trunk transitions while retaining the accepted crown and foliage. Root crossings are intentional stylized contacts reviewed at normal viewing scale, not proof of universally disjoint roots. Plant dimensions, counts, pose and palette are artistic, not a specimen cast or measured individual.
属级解释性生活树蕨复原，呈现连续树干、渐细根套及分裂蕨叶。本次有限修订改善根与干的过渡，保留已验收冠形和叶片。根系交接为正常观看尺度下已审阅的风格化接触，并不表示所有根均互不相交；尺寸、数量、姿态及配色属美术选择，不是标本翻模或实测个体。
