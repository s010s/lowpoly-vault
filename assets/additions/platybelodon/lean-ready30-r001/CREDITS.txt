This adult-male Platybelodon grangeri has a broad shovel-shaped lower jaw, paired flattened lower tusks, separate upper tusks and a flexible trunk. The head follows identified fossils, while body and soft tissues are artistic estimates; mud-shovelling is not treated as established behaviour.
此成年雄性铲齿象 Platybelodon grangeri 具有宽铲状下颌、成对扁平下门齿、独立上门齿和柔软的鼻。头部参照已鉴定化石，躯体与软组织为艺术估计；不把铲泥视为已确定行为。
