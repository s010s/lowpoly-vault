This deep-bodied bird with a massive compressed beak, strong legs and tiny wings represents Diatryma gigantea under the historical Gastornis catalogue name. It follows the 2024 distinction between these genera; feathers, colour, soft tissues and pose are artistic inferences.
这只体躯厚重、具有巨大侧扁喙、强壮双腿和小翅的鸟，以目录中的历史名称 Gastornis 对应 Diatryma gigantea 进行复原。模型采用 2024 年对这两个属的区分；羽被、颜色、软组织与姿态为艺术推断。
