Diprotodon optatum has a deep wombat-like torso, high shoulders and a broad fleshy muzzle. Fur, ears and nasal soft tissue are inferred; a short trunk remains debated, and this reconstruction has no long proboscis.
双门齿兽 Diprotodon optatum 具有厚重的袋熊状躯干、高肩和宽厚肉质吻部。毛被、耳部及鼻部软组织为推断；短鼻的存在仍有争议，本复原不采用长鼻。
