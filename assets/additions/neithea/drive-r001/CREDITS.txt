Original low-poly Neithea grandicosta-informed fossil shell, with six main ribs, a convex right valve and a flatter left valve. Pairing, thickness and color are artistic. Paired valves combine different specimens; thickness, hinge, palette and upright pose are artistic, not an exact articulated specimen.
