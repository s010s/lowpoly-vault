Embolotherium andrewsi with a broad frontonasal ram and reconstructed enlarged nasal soft tissue, informed by the type skull and AMNH interpretation. The body is a comparative brontothere reconstruction, not an associated skeleton; skin, ears, proportions and scale are illustrative, without weapon-function or vocal-behaviour claims.
雷兽 Embolotherium andrewsi 参考模式头骨与 AMNH 解释，呈现宽大额鼻突及推定膨大的鼻部软组织。躯体为比较解剖复原，非完整关联骨架；皮肤、耳、比例与尺度为示意，不宣称武器功能或发声行为。
