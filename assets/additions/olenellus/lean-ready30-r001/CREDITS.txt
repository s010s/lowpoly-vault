This genus-level Olenellus has a broad crescent-shaped head, long backward cheek spines and a narrow segmented trunk ending in a small tail shield. Colour, body depth and hidden anatomy are interpretive, not directly preserved details.
此属级 Olenellus 具有宽阔月牙形头部、向后伸长的颊刺和末端带小尾甲的狭长分节躯干。颜色、体厚及隐藏部位的解剖结构属于解释性复原，并非直接保存的细节。
