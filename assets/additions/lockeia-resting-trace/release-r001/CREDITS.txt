Trace-morphology teaching concept showing one shallow almond-shaped negative resting impression with pointed ends in an interpretive sediment crop. Not a shell, body fossil, named tracemaker or copied excavated specimen. Nominal 20 cm crop span, relief and palette are artistic choices; the crop is not an inferred organism size.
