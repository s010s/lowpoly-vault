Original low-poly fossil-form interpretation of Paraspirifer, with a deep biconvex shell, median dorsal fold and opposing ventral sulcus. Approximately 40 mm wide; hidden depth is an artistic estimate. Approximately 40 mm width is specimen-anchored; hidden depth, posterior, color and ribs are inferred. This new candidate does not recover or inherit the lost historical revision.
