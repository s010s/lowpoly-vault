# Reviewed native twenty-five — 2026-10-29

25 independently accepted original models selected for integration. GLB and editable Blender sources retain the accepted selected bytes. The 50 paired website images were captured from those exact GLBs using the existing website renderers, then independently reviewed. All 25 pairs passed with nonblocking observations: fine Seirocrinus branches and a few tight shadow margins. This image review does not repeat or replace scientific model acceptance.

## Shared limits

Model-space units are meters for scene interoperability, but dimensions are authored display dimensions, often normalized to approximately 1 m. They are **not measured biological sizes**. Fossil specimens and living reconstructions are distinguished below. Reference images and papers are consultation sources only and are not included as textures or downloadable media. No universal GPU, composed-scene, watertight topology or biological precision claim is made.

Nyctosaurus retains its original editable geometry source plus the accepted R1 index-only serialization correction; the historical exact-export mismatch remains a recorded failure. Its optional `normalize_indices.py` documents the deterministic correction, not a new generation.

## Model scopes and references

### Walliserops trifurcatus inspired living reconstruction

Gishlick and Fortey (2023), figures 1A (UA13447) and 2A (HMNS PI1810). Underside limbs, shell depth, soft articulation and color are inferred; no specimen-exact spine count, biological size or combat claim.

[Consulted source](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9942788/)

### Trinucleus genus-level living reconstruction

Museum Wales Ordovician trilobite guide, bottom-right Trinucleus only. Broad perforated fringe and segmented trunk are stylized; pit count, limb count, underside, depth and color are inferred. No species-level identification.

[Consulted source](https://museum.wales/media/55804/26SG-Ordovician-Trilobites-of-Mid-Wales-2.pdf)

### Comura inspired living reconstruction

Oxford University Museum specimen captioned Comura bultyncki informs a genus-level reconstruction. Exact spine count, hidden side, soft anatomy, color and depth are not recovered measurements. Specimen restoration history and catalog number are unspecified.

[Consulted source](https://oumnh.web.ox.ac.uk/learn-fossil-invertebrates)

### Borealopelta inspired living reconstruction

Brown et al. (2020), holotype TMP2011.033.0001, figure 1a/b anchors preserved anterior armor. Figure 1c is extrapolated and the Kunbarrasaurus panel is excluded. Rear body, tail, limbs, digits and living coloration remain reconstruction.

[Consulted source](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7353971/)

### Carnotaurus sastrei inspired living reconstruction

NHMLA skeletal mount and CONICET reconstruction inform the horned skull and whole body. Soft tissues, coloration and stance remain artistic; subdued texture is not a claim of high-resolution fossil skin scales. R2 fixes texture-coordinate binding while retaining accepted R1 geometry.

[Consulted source](https://nhm.org/stories/jurassic-perks)

### Hydnoceras genus-level living reconstruction

West Virginia Geological and Economic Survey museum specimen 294, Hydnoceras sp., is the genus-level anchor. Living depth, attachment, openings and coloration are bounded interpretation, not a scan or narrower species identification.

[Consulted source](https://www.wvgs.wvnet.edu/www/museum/musenew3.htm)

### Seirocrinus subangularis inspired living reconstruction

Smithsonian USNM PAL242239 anchors the curved articulated stalk and branching crown. The flattened fossil fan is interpreted as a three-dimensional living cup; depth, exact branching, attachment and color are inferred. R2 is the selected lighter 95,192-triangle artifact, not a many-object performance guarantee; closed branches overlap rather than forming a welded organism.

[Consulted source](https://artsandculture.google.com/asset/crinoid-fossil/ggEcGx3jk-A6og?hl=en)

### Coeloptychium agaricoides inspired living reconstruction

Natural History Museum London Cretaceous fossil informs the folded cap and central form. Fold count, living depth, stalk/base length and coloration are assumptions; this is a stylized living sponge rather than an exact fossil restoration.

[Consulted source](https://www.nhm.ac.uk/our-science/services/collections/palaeontology/fossil-sponges.html)

### Procoptodon goliah inspired living reconstruction

Naracoorte Caves educational guide pages 8–9, lower-left Procoptodon illustration and skull views; upper Diprotodon skeletons are excluded. Soft tissues, eyelids, fur, pose and proportions are interpretive, not calibrated fossil measurements.

[Consulted source](https://cdn.environment.sa.gov.au/naracoortecaves/docs/naracoorte-caves-animals-and-fossils-fact.pdf)

### Tainoceras monilifer fossil interpretation

Digital Atlas of Ancient Life three-view fossil, caption PRI42134. The visible K1404 marking differs from that caption; the discrepancy is unresolved and markings are omitted. Compact whorls, shoulder nodes, mineral-filled umbilicus and broken terminal are stylized; hidden anatomy is not reconstructed as living tissue.

[Consulted source](https://www.digitalatlasofancientlife.org/learn/mollusca/cephalopoda/nautiloidea/)

### Leptaena richmondensis fossil interpretation

Atlas of Ordovician Life OUIP2232, Liberty Formation, Indiana. A simplified concavo-convex closed shell with concentric rugae and peripheral downturn; the hidden opposing valve depth is inferred. Relief is more regular and ventral rugae more subdued than the worn fossil. No living soft parts.

[Consulted source](https://www.ordovicianatlas.org/atlas/brachiopoda/strophomenata/strophomenida/rafinesquinidae/leptaena/leptaena-richmondensis/)

### Spinatrypa spinosa fossil interpretation

Devonian Atlas PRI109509 is the sole selected specimen anchor. Historical S. bunkeri identification conflict is unresolved and excluded; PRI109534 anatomy is not used. Ribs, interrupted growth lips and worn low spine bases are stylized; hidden depth is inferred, not scan metrology.

[Consulted source](https://devonianatlas.org/species/spinatrypa-spinosa/)

### Cyphaspis eximia inspired living reconstruction

Trilolab author-team photograph anchors the three long swept spines and tuberculate head. Exact counts, underside, depth, color and biological size are not established. Texture binds TEXCOORD_1; projected UVs collapse on some vertical rims (about 2.96% of surface), accepted only for this low-frequency pigment, not a detailed-texture unwrap.

[Consulted source](https://www.trilolab.net/mor-cyphaspis.html)

### Scaphites fossil interpretation

Smithsonian Q?rius specimen 55436, Upper Cretaceous Wyoming. The irregular broken mineralized region is deliberately coarse low-poly interpretation; reverse surfaces and thickness are inferred. Museum size approximately 3 × 2.8 × 1.3 cm is distinct from the artistically normalized model; no living anatomy or pristine aperture is claimed.

[Consulted source](https://qrius.si.edu/browse/object/10023051)

### Orbiculoidea baini fossil interpretation

Museums Victoria P52629, Early Devonian Falkland Islands, anchors the shallow irregular disc and displaced growth center. Underside and profile are inferred from one source view. Restrained grey lamellae are intentionally low contrast, not recovered fossil pigmentation.

[Consulted source](https://collections.museumsvictoria.com.au/specimens/1353268)

### Leonaspis burketti inspired living reconstruction

MNHNL holotype QC440 public photogrammetry thumbnail is consulted as an image only; no source mesh is incorporated. Long anterior and shorter posterior marginal spines are qualitative. Exact spine counts, hidden side, depth, underside limbs and color are inferred.

[Consulted source](https://data.public.lu/en/datasets/3d-reconstruction-of-leonaspis-burketti-qc440-holotype-via-photogrammetry/)

### Cupressocrinites genus-level living reconstruction

Smithsonian record nmnhpaleobiology_12133328 and Treatise figure 430 2a–d anchor five massive tapering unbranched arms and low cup. Visible marking 58358 is not promoted to a verified catalog prefix. Lower stalk, simple attachment, arm opening and color are inferred; no branching frond anatomy.

[Consulted source](https://www.si.edu/object/nmnhpaleobiology_12133328)

### Arctodus simus inspired living reconstruction

Ohio History Connection skeleton/life reconstruction and Illinois State Museum diorama anchor A. simus, not the older inventory A. pristinus reference. Soft tissues, fur, eyes and exact proportions are interpretive. R4 is selected; the reference resolution does not support precise biological ratios.

[Consulted source](https://www.ohiohistory.org/a-giant-bear-once-lived-in-ohio/)

### Crucibulum springvaleense fossil interpretation

Florida Museum UF208841, Crucibulum (Dispotaea) springvaleense. The museum page heading spells spingvaleense while image naming uses springvaleense; no taxonomic correction is implied. Hollow cap, radial relief and attached internal cup are stylized; shell depth and smoother cup outline are inferred.

[Consulted source](https://www.floridamuseum.ufl.edu/panama-pire/fossils/gatun/crucibulum-dispotaea-spingvaleense/)

### Productus productus fossil interpretation

Treatise figure 313 1a–e, holotype BMNH B40952, informs the corpus and thin curved anterior trail. Precise preserved trail boundary and opposing valve anatomy remain uncertain. No long invented spines, soft tissue, surrounding matrix or exact-specimen claim.

[Consulted source](https://brachiopod.treatise.geolex.org/displayInfo.php?genera=Productus)

### Symphysurus ebbestadi inspired living reconstruction

Drage et al. (2019), figure 1A, MGL102127 anchors the broad head, eight thoracic segments and substantial rounded tailshield. Provenance correction: MGL102127 is a putative moulted exoskeleton, NOT a hardened inter-moult carcass. Living profile, underside limbs, antennae and color are inferred; fossil depth and limb counts are not measured.

[Consulted source](https://www.app.pan.pl/archive/published/app64/app005822018.pdf)

### Edmontonia rugosidens inspired living reconstruction

Scott Hartman whole-body skeletal drawing (2011, revised 2024) and AMNH shoulder photograph are consultation references only. Shoulder photograph catalog identity is unverified. Rear anatomy, soft tissue, digits, pigmentation and pose remain interpretive; broad support pads are stylized. No reference art is redistributed.

[Consulted source](https://www.skeletaldrawing.com/ornithischians/edmontonia-rugosidens/)

### Lystrosaurus murrayi inspired living reconstruction

Dmitry Bogdanov life reconstruction cited by UW Biology and Smithsonian USNM V12687 skull photographs are distinct artistic and fossil references. Species scope is L. murrayi, not L. maccaigi. Soft tissues, keratin beak surface, pigment and stance are inferred.

[Consulted source](https://depts.washington.edu/scipos/wordpress/2021/10/20/summer-summary-series-zoe-kulik-and-400-fossil-skulls/)

### Nyctosaurus gracilis inspired living reconstruction

NixIllustration (2020) soaring reconstruction and AMNH crest evidence inform a crested adult with bare fork. A spanning sail is not modeled; absence of crest soft tissue is not proven. Wing camber, lower surfaces, feet, color and keratin thickness are inferred. R1 changes only opaque eye triangle index ordering; original editable Blend remains the geometry source, with deterministic serialization postprocess. Historical unchanged-export byte mismatch remains a HOLD, not retrospectively passed.

[Consulted source](https://nixillustration.com/science-illustration/2020/weird-heads-month-01-nyctosaurus/)

### Koolasuchus cleelandi inspired living reconstruction

Museums Victoria reconstruction based on Andrew Plant and Trusler anterior artwork inform the broad low head and whole body. Digits, underside, coloration and soft tissue remain inferred. Mild shoulder/hip shading creases and a few zero-area triangles are retained nonblocking technical limitations, not a perfect-manifold claim.

[Consulted source](https://museumsvictoria.com.au/melbournemuseum/deep-dives/victorias-fossil-treasures/)
