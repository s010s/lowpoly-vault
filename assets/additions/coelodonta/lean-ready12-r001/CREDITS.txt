Whole-adult woolly rhinoceros, Coelodonta antiquitatis, combining fossil and permafrost evidence with a substantial oval nasal-horn base. Proportions are approximate, not a cast of one specimen; fur, colour, soft tissue, pose and full horn curvature remain artistic choices.
披毛犀 Coelodonta antiquitatis 成体全身复原，综合化石与冻土材料，鼻角采用厚实椭圆基部。体比例为近似值，非单一标本翻模；毛量、颜色、软组织、姿势与完整角弯度仍属美术选择。
