American mastodon, Mammut americanum, with a low elongated head, stocky body and gently upcurved upper tusks, informed by several museum skeletons. Soft tissues, coat and scale are illustrative. Lower tusks are omitted for this adult interpretation, not declared absent throughout the species; diagnostic molars are not exposed.
美洲乳齿象 Mammut americanum 综合多具博物馆骨架，呈现低长头部、粗壮躯体及平缓上弯的上颌长牙。软组织、毛被与尺度为示意。本成体复原不显示下颌长牙，不代表全物种均无此结构；鉴别性臼齿未外露。
