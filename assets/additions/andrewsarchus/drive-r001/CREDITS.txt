Original low-poly representation of the long, broad type upper cranium AMNH 20135, retaining its one-sided cheek-arch loss. Upper cranium only, no mandible or body; artistic 0.85 m envelope, not verified 834/560 mm dimensions. Teeth and hidden anatomy are simplified, not diagnostic replicas.
