Original elongated inflated paired fossil shell with continuous sweeping radial ribs and a natural posterior opening, informed by P. lirata. Multiple P. lirata specimens inform the form; nominal 90 mm length, height, inflation, shell thickness and gape width are artistic, not measured single-specimen anatomy.
