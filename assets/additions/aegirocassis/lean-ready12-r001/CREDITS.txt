Fossil-informed Aegirocassis with two flap tiers and paired filtering appendages using the revised six-long-endite interpretation. Eyes, soft tissues, colour, pose and exact proportions are reconstructed; filtering setae are reduced and thickened, not a true mesh-size model. No tail fan is added.
依据化石复原的 Aegirocassis，保留双层侧叶及成对滤食附肢，采用六条长内叶的新解释。眼、软组织、颜色、姿态与精确比例属推定；滤食刚毛减少并加粗，不代表真实滤网孔径，不添加尾扇。
