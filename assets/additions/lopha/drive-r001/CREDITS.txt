Original low-poly folded oyster fossil form, historically called Lopha, informed by Actinostreon marshii. Unequal complete valves, broad attachment region, irregular radial folds and a geometric zigzag commissure. Historical Lopha name; Actinostreon marshii-informed art, not exact specimen replication. Pairing, depth, unseen geometry and color are inferred.
