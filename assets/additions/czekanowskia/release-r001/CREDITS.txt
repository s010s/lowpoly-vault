Stylized preserved foliage-organ teaching specimen: grouped narrow leaves repeatedly fork from a compact short shoot. No whole crown, reproductive linkage, exact species or museum specimen is reconstructed. Leaf arrangement, thickness, palette and nominal 18 cm foliage span are artistic choices rather than fossil measurements.
