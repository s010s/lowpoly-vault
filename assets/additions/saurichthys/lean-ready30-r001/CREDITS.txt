Saurichthys curionii has a needlelike body, long opposing jaws and rear-set dorsal and anal fins. Fossil compression leaves living body breadth, soft-tissue outlines, exact fin rays and colour uncertain.
Saurichthys curionii 具有针状细长身体、上下相对的长颌，以及靠近尾部的背鳍和臀鳍。化石受压使活体体宽、软组织轮廓、精确鳍条及颜色仍有不确定性。
