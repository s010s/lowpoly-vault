Adult Pentecopterus decorahensis with differentiated limbs, compound paddles and a blade-like telson, reconstructed from compressed exuviae. The unpreserved dorsal shield, eyes and metastoma are hypothetical; depth, pose and colour are artistic. The 0.85 m body-length target is a representative adult estimate, not a complete specimen or exceptional maximum; sex remains uncertain.
成体 Pentecopterus decorahensis 依据受压蜕壳复原，保留分化附肢、复合游泳桨与刀片状尾剑。未保存的背盾、眼及口后板属假说；体厚、姿态与颜色为美术选择。0.85米体长目标为代表性成体估计，非完整标本实测或最大值；性别不确定。
