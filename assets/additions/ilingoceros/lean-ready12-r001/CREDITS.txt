Spiral-horned Ilingoceros reconstruction centred on the I. alexandrae horn-core morphotype. No associated complete skeleton is established; body, size, sheath thickness, exact twist and forked tips are interpretive. Species relationships remain unresolved, and the tips are not claimed preserved on the selected type fragment.
以 I. alexandrae 角心形态为中心的螺旋角 Ilingoceros 复原。未证实完整关联骨架；躯体、尺寸、角鞘厚度、具体扭转及分叉角尖为解释性补全。种级关系仍未解决，不声称所选模式残片保存完整角尖。
