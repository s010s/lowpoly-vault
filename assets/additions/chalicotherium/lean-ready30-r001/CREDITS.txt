This genus-level Chalicotherium has long powerful clawed forelimbs, shorter hindlimbs and a horse-like head. Whole-body form is partly based on an interpretive skeletal illustration; soft tissues and the knuckle-supported stance are reconstructed, not directly observed.
此属级爪兽 Chalicotherium 具有强壮修长的带爪前肢、较短后肢和马状头部。全身形态部分依据解释性骨架绘图；软组织及指节支撑姿态属于复原，而非直接观察所得。
