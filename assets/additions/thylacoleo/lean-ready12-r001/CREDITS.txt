Thylacoleo carnifex, the marsupial lion, in a neutral whole-body stance based on composite fossil anatomy. Soft tissues, coat, ears, colour and size are interpretive; the missing hind-foot hallux tip is conservatively completed. Climbing and tail-bracing remain functional hypotheses, not observed behaviour or a depicted hunting event.
