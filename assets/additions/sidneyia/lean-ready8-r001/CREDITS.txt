Sidneyia inexpectans. Compression fossils do not preserve life volume or color. This revision adopts the 2023 interpretation of three post-antennal head-limb pairs and nine trunk-limb pairs; the strongest direct new head evidence is from S. minor, supported by reinterpretation of S. inexpectans specimens. Abdominal counts of two and three remain published alternatives: r001 explicitly follows the two-sclerite Burgess Shale interpretation in Zacaï et al. 2016. Exact limb joint and gill-filament counts, shell thickness, soft tissues, posture and palette are simplified or inferred, not fossil-exact.
