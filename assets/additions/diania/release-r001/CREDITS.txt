Stylized living reconstruction inspired by Diania: a thin continuous trunk above paired robust spiny lobopods. Pose, reduced ornament, limb presentation, palette and nominal 6 cm scale are teaching-art choices, not a measured fossil or exact species restoration. No resolved arthropod ancestry or rigid joint/claw anatomy is asserted.
以 Diania 为启发的风格化生活复原：细长连续躯干配成对粗壮多刺叶足。姿态、简化纹饰、肢体表现、配色及名义6厘米尺度属教学美术选择，并非实测化石或精确种级复原；不主张已确定的节肢动物祖先关系、硬关节或爪结构。
