Cheirolepis trailli has a large oblique gape, tapered scaled trunk and unequal tail lobes. Body thickness, fin margins, colour and dimensions are estimates informed by a flattened fossil photograph and published restorations.
Cheirolepis trailli 具有斜向张开的大口、收尖的有鳞躯干和不等大的尾鳍叶。体厚、鳍缘、颜色及尺寸参照受压化石照片和已发表复原图进行估计。
