Palaeoniscum freieslebeni has an elongate diamond-scaled trunk and a strongly asymmetric tail with a longer upper axis. Body breadth, missing fin edges and colour are inferred from compressed fossils; scale rows are simplified rather than exact counts.
Palaeoniscum freieslebeni 具有修长的菱形鳞片躯干，以及上部轴线较长的明显不对称尾鳍。体宽、缺失鳍缘与颜色由受压化石推断；鳞列经过简化，并非精确计数。
