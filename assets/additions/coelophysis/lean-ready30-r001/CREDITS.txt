Coelophysis bauri has a slender body, long neck, narrow toothed muzzle and tapering balancing tail. Soft tissues, colour, posture and body width are estimated; compressed fossils and restored casts do not establish exact living dimensions.
腔骨龙 Coelophysis bauri 具有纤细身体、长颈、狭窄带齿吻部和收尖的平衡尾。软组织、颜色、姿态及体宽为估计；受压化石与修复铸型不能确定精确活体尺寸。
