# Cobbania stalk-junction material repair

The exact live floating-plant stylization has readable leaves and hanging roots. Its dark leaf-to-stalk junction hides the stem branches and root-seat connection from some angles. An isolated 1 / 0.8 / 0.65 comparison showed that 0.8 restores useful separation at the junction without washing out the leaf tops or removing the shaded undersides. Initial diagnostic variants changed both materials and were superseded by the single-material control.

- The leaf/stalk textured material at index 0 changes default metallic factor 1 → 0.8. The named manually authored tan-root material is unchanged, including explicit metallic 0. Remaining GLB JSON and BIN are identical. Geometry, UVs, pose, authored scale and texture unchanged; no reconstruction.
- Production GLB/Blend/preview/left poster/right poster match baseline bytes. Original and candidate validator report zero errors and zero warnings. Candidate editable source was imported from its exact GLB and reopened with packed images; its geometry, matrices, UV and nonempty texture bytes/pixels match the original and candidate.
- Both matching posters were regenerated through the current PreviewRenderer. Desktop 1440 × 1000 and 390 × 844 simulated touch right-detail endpoints saw no obvious shift, scale, pose, color or texture jump. Original samples 20/45, candidate 34/46, with no timestamp reversals or page errors. Both variants passed all eight lifecycle checks, four actual GLB/Blend button downloads and both actual poster-response hashes.
- Scoped independent review only under controlled GLB delay, not real-device or every-frame certification. Unified branch CI and production release are separate.
