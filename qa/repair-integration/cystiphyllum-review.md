# Cystiphyllum material-only review

The live GLB, Blend, preview and two gallery posters matched Git. At 90°, 180° and 270° on a fixed camera and light setup, the fully metallic default obscured extensive bands around the cup. Reducing the metallic factor from 1 to 0.65 restores readable wall and lip relief while preserving sandy brown and natural cavity shadows. Other GLB JSON and the binary chunk match exactly; geometry, textures, transforms, authored size and default pose are unchanged. Blender source was derived from this exact candidate, not represented as the original authoring project; reopened Blend matched geometry, UVs, matrices and the nonempty 1024² packed texture. Original and candidate GLBs validate without warnings or errors.

Source-versioned wheel and detail images were captured with the matching renderer and pinned by exact hash; the original did not have authored wheelSpec metadata, and none was added. In a scoped production-code build, the withheld-GLB poster and settled native rendering showed no noticeable right-side shape, position, scale, direction or material jump at 1440 desktop and 390 simulated mobile in independent endpoint review. Candidate CDP sampling yielded 30 desktop and 47 mobile frames without page errors; one mobile timestamp inversion was observed, also seen in the original run. These are finite samples, not all-frame or physical-device coverage. Eight interaction cases, four actual GLB/Blend downloads and both poster HTTP pins passed. Unified branch CI and production release remain pending.
