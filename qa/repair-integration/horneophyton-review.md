# Horneophyton material-only review

The live GLB, Blend, preview and two gallery posters matched Git. In the controlled four-angle comparison the default fully metallic surface obscured the stems and capsule connections, especially near 90° and 270°. Changing metallic factor from 1 to 0.65 restores readable depth and green/brown tones without erasing shadows. Other GLB JSON and the binary chunk are identical: geometry, textures, transforms, authored size and default pose remain unchanged. The candidate editable source was derived from this exact GLB, not represented as the source authoring project; reopened Blend matched geometry, UVs, matrices and the nonempty 1024² packed texture. Original and candidate GLBs validate without warnings or errors.

The gallery was tested in a scoped production-code build with the actual model and a separate anchor. In independent review of the withheld-GLB poster and settled native rendering, the right-side stems and capsules showed no noticeable shape, position, scale, direction or colour jump at desktop 1440 and simulated mobile 390. Candidate sampling yielded 29 and 46 frames respectively, with no out-of-order timestamps or page errors. These are finite samples, not physical devices or continuous-frame coverage. All eight interaction cases passed, plus four real GLB/Blend downloads and both poster HTTP pins. Scope is a material repair and exact-version browser/delivery review, not a scientific reinterpretation. Unified branch CI and production release remain pending.
