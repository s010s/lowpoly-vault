# Salvinia: single-value material adjustment

Same-camera controls at four azimuths plus an unlit source-color reference show that the original glTF metallic default of 1 suppresses readable green and detail at the stolon connections, leaf veins, and hanging filamentous leaves. The .65 control restores detail without removing the natural darker leaf undersides. No geometry, pose, fit, lighting, or texture changes.

- Original GLB SHA-256 14ff6fa0cfadd14e100f0d7fc9f508bbf21993568b226d7d0dd02356b796123b; candidate 2db89902250ea8157f61b66942ee90a53a20ea64506ccecdbd8c0bd255e9ea55. Only one GLB JSON PBR value was added; other JSON and the complete BIN chunk are unchanged. Paired native packed Blender source received the same single unlinked value adjustment; reopening and comparing to the candidate import matched meshes, matrices, UVs and image pixels/packed data. Neither GLB had validator errors or warnings.
- Wheel and detail PNGs were redrawn from the exact candidate. Actual held-GLB handoffs at 1440×1000 and emulated 390×844 show consistent endpoint pose, apparent size and material. Frame sampling is finite; this is not a physical-device or continuous frame-order claim. Source-bound poster responses, four actual downloads per version, regenerated ZIP/credits and scoped browser interactions were exercised.
- The existing shared gallery layout remains open: at emulated 370×710 EN the tool group starts at y=456 CSS px and covers 151 of 23,117 projected vertices of the distal filamentous tips, whose projection extends to y=468.34. The original and revised material give the identical result; heading, interaction button and download card do not intersect projected vertices. No per-model workaround was added; shared layout integration and full branch CI are not covered by this material approval.
