# Montlivaltia material-only review

The live GLB, Blend, preview and two gallery posters matched Git. Under a fixed camera and light setup, the fully metallic default obscured much of the lower cup-wall relief. Reducing the metallic factor from 1 to 0.65 restores band and lip readability while preserving sandy brown and natural cavity shadows. Other GLB JSON and the binary chunk match exactly; geometry, base colour textures, transforms, authored size and default pose are unchanged. Blender source was derived from this exact candidate, not represented as the original authoring project; reopening it verified matching geometry, UVs, matrices and nonempty packed texture. Both GLBs validate without warnings or errors.

Exact source-versioned wheel and detail images were captured with the matching renderer and hash-pinned. In a scoped production-code build, the withheld-GLB poster and settled native rendering showed no noticeable right-side shape, position, scale, direction or material jump at 1440 desktop and 390 simulated mobile in independent endpoint review. Candidate CDP sampling returned 34 desktop and 47 mobile frames without page errors; one desktop timestamp inversion was observed (the original run had two inversions on mobile). These are finite samples, not all-frame or physical-device coverage. Eight interaction cases, four actual GLB/Blend downloads and both poster HTTP pins passed. Unified branch CI and production release remain pending.
