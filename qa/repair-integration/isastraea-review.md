# Isastraea: single-value material adjustment

The original upper cup outlines are readable, but several lower neighboring cups visually merge into the darkest part of the model under the default glTF metallic value of 1. Same-camera native-shader controls at four azimuths and an unlit source-color control confirmed two focused improvements at metallic factor .65: at 90°, the adjacent lower-left cup edges and several radial septa separate; at 180°, the lower-central shared edges read separately and adjacent septa become discernible. The earth-brown shell and recess shadows remain; 0/.35 were visibly paler. No retouching of texture, geometry, cups or holes, material lighting, or orientation.
