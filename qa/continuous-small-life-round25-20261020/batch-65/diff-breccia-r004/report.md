## Differential finding

**Source/model differential: ACCEPT BOUNDED.** The independently reopened r004 source and exact-export renders preserve the frozen teaching target: coherent dark matrix, many differently sized visibly angular contrasting clasts on top and sides, no obvious loose pile, and no conglomerate-like rounded pebble field. I personally inspected all eight individual 512×512 r004 manifest-bound views (`hero`, `reversehero`, `front`, `side`, `rear`, `top`, `clay`, `silhouette`) and verified their byte hashes/dimensions against the manifest. These CPU proof views do not show the fine, repeated near-parallel crosshatching seen in the retained r003 website HOLD screenshots. The silhouette and clast field remain recognizable. Several clasts are slabby/protruding and occur in localized trains; that is a visual caveat, but they remain angular and embedded rather than reading as rounded loose gravel.

Independent exact-GLB triangle witness test: r003 had 502 qualifying near-parallel projected triangle pairs, r004 has 0 at the broad witness setting (normal dot ≥ 0.999999, plane separation ≤ 1 mm, positive projected area ≥ 1e-6 m²); strict 10 µm results were 0→0. The producer’s detector reported 499→0. All 499 producer pairs occur in the independent set; the three additional independent cases are boundary inclusions under the same stated tolerances. This is a narrow geometry diagnostic, not proof of website pixels or a complete collision rule.

A separate independent convex-hull/SAT test found actual clast-solid volume intersections in both revisions: r003 273 positive-volume pairs; r004 68 (out of 120 clasts). Clast mesh/hull volume ratios were 0.9999994–1.0000000, so the convex SAT comparison is applicable. These residual pairs are real 3D overlaps, not just touching; minimum overlap-axis depth ranged 0.000692–0.18 m (median 0.0842 m). This count is not interchangeable with the near-parallel triangle witness count and is not treated as a newly invented universal pass/fail gate. The eight source frames show no r003-like thin crosshatching, but they cannot prove every residual overlap is pixel-invisible in the website renderer. No claim of zero clast-clast solid intersection is made; the fresh website capture must still test the actual symptom.

The repair is a genuine geometry edit rather than a material/render concealment.
