## Asset-specific visual/factual/specification findings

- **Dinomischus r002 — ACCEPT bounded.** The 32 inspected views show a complete attached organism: basal bulb, slender rigid stem, open shallow cup and the full crown of 20 stiff, flattened, upright bracts. This is not a shell or narrowed partial. The ROM’s primary description supports the cup, stem, bulb and 20 upright bracts; exact affinities are explicitly uncertain. Subtle upper apertures are identified as interpretations, not settled anatomy. Official source: [Royal Ontario Museum, Dinomischus isolatus](https://burgess-shale.rom.on.ca/fossils/dinomischus-isolatus/).
- **Star dune r002 — ACCEPT bounded.** Single continuous closed sand volume. Top/overhead views show five unequal radiating ridges connecting at the common summit and descending into concave inter-ridge saddles; not disconnected tent-like piles. The NPS defines star dunes as having three or more ridges descending from a summit. Scale is stated as illustrative, not a measurement or locality replica. Official source: [NPS, Dune Types](https://www.nps.gov/grsa/learn/nature/dune-types.htm).
- **Slot canyon r001 — ACCEPT bounded.** Top and side/front/rear views show an actual continuous narrow sinuous open passage between deep facing walls, with substrate and fluted erosion; passage is not a shallow decorated gully. The deliberate outer crop and open ends are disclosed; this is not a whole named canyon. The source describes slot canyons as deep and narrow and canyon forms as steep-sided erosion. Official sources: [NPS, Burro Wash](https://www.nps.gov/care/planyourvisit/burrowash.htm) and [NPS, Arid Landforms](https://www.nps.gov/subjects/geology/arid-landforms.htm).
- **Flowstone cascade r002 — ACCEPT bounded.** All views show a solid connected mineral sheet with relief descending across three uneven ledges, fluting and broad spreading lower apron; two ledge lips carry 18 rounded tongues. It is not a string of detached beads or merely painted stripes; reverse/rear views expose host-backed volume. The brief correctly describes generic carbonate deposition and makes no liquid waterfall/site-replica claim. Official source: [NPS, Speleothems](https://www.nps.gov/subjects/caves/speleothems.htm).
