# Source and rights

This technical-exhibit mannequin is a derivative of MakeHuman Community's core body and anatomical pose assets. The detailed face was replaced with an original featureless head; the body was simplified, reposed, and given a sparse topology overlay. This version broadens the head slightly and uses solid light-gray materials with directional studio shading.

MakeHuman's core graphical assets, including its basemesh, skeleton and weights, are released under CC0 1.0. The original basemesh explicitly states its CC0 release in September 2020; the skeleton and weight files declare CC0. The listed original holders are Data Collection AB, Joel Palmius and Jonas Hauquier.
