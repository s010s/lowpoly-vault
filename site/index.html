LOWPOLY VAULT

Good shapes.
Open files.

An open library of 1,000 original, editable low-poly 3D models by Leon Made This / s010s. Free for personal and commercial projects under CC BY 4.0 — download ready-to-use GLB files or open the Blender sources to remix them.

Free for commercial use

Use, adapt, and ship in personal or commercial projects under CC BY 4.0. Just credit Leon Made This / s010s, link the license, and note changes.

GLB + editable Blender files

Every model includes a web- and engine-ready GLB alongside its editable .blend source file and attribution text.
