# Practical stylized environment props

Recovered policy intent, 2026-10-18. This is a reconstruction of the agreed 2026-10-17 scope, not a claim of byte-identical recovery or reused QA.

Use `profile: production` plus `quality_tier: environment-prop` for static, stylized-inspired supporting assets in the imaginative world for young children. This scope permits creative colors, textures, appealing poses, proportion exaggeration and harmless fine details. Judge basic recognizability and convincing major structure at normal supported viewing size, rather than demanding strict scientific reconstruction or microscopic perfection.

## Two roles

- Environment prop: normally about 80–240 projected pixels; pilot closest supported inspection about 360 pixels. Small physical size does not automatically mean easy anatomy or low repeated-scene cost.
- Hero/close inspection: a separately requested focal role with its own stronger view/reference/surface brief. Do not apply it retroactively to every supporting prop.

Use “Stylized <taxon>” when basic identity reads. Use a descriptive name plus “inspired by <taxon>” for a deliberately loose interpretation. One or a few representative references are enough to start; record what was accessed and what is creative. Fine fossil uncertainty is advisory unless core identity is unclear. Specific scientific claims still need evidence; the prop tier does not convert an unknown into a fact. Cross-period curation is permitted in a clearly labeled imagined world, without a scientific coexistence claim.

References do not grant reuse rights. Make original geometry/maps and keep unlicensed photos, figures, scans and source models out of delivered assets.

## Six to eight useful images

Default to hero/front/right/rear/top/clay, at modest resolution and the supported scale. Add one or two targeted views only for a real concern. The hero is also the one website poster; detail uses the real GLB. Set `turntable_frames: 0` with a coverage reason. No default orbit, bottom/wireframe campaign or HD polishing cycle. Existing briefs without the explicit prop tier retain their frozen coverage.

The standard renderer accepts `--turntable-frames 0 --views hero,front,right,rear,top,clay --resolution 512 --samples 8`. Inspect actual pixels; zoom only to classify a visible problem. Source renders do not prove the exported GLB works.

## Blockers versus advisories

Fix corrupt exports, missing dependencies, nonfinite geometry, wrong scale/origin, clearly broken materials, unreadable identity, obvious accidental extra/missing major appendages, impossible attachments, conspicuous unwanted penetration, rights issues, stale evidence and exceeded hard budgets.

Minor hue/roughness, facet/seam variation, subpixel detail, labeled fossil uncertainty and harmless artistic choices are advisories. Record tradeoffs within the brief's tolerance rather than inventing a new hard gate. Deliberate invisible overlap may be documented as an implementation choice.

## Bounded independent review

One fresh reviewer per candidate may cover visual/basic-reference/specification checks together. No self-review and no per-stage committee. Consolidate findings once; permit at most one planned cosmetic repair, then accept with advisories if no blocker remains. Real hard defects receive targeted repair and affected retests; unresolved blockers can be parked while other assets progress.

No per-model user approval is required. Ask only for material scope/style/cost decisions, conflicting constraints or new authority. Keep automated export checks, actual hashes, original-content rights and honest loader/graphics/composed-scene results. One pass can collect several stages; unknown mandatory runtime evidence is not a pass. Neutral benchmarks do not certify current-museum integration. No rig or animation-ready claim is included without separate motion tests.
