# Traffic-signal factual brief example

Freeze jurisdiction, era/standard edition, vehicular/pedestrian/cycle/rail type, circular/arrow indications, vertical/horizontal/cluster orientation, intended face direction, mounting, dimensions and illuminated state or animation scope. An unspecified “realistic traffic light” cannot be approved against one assumed global standard.

For a selected U.S. three-section circular vehicular face, FHWA's current publication checked on 17 October 2026 is the [MUTCD 11th Edition with Revision 1, December 2025](https://mutcd.fhwa.dot.gov/kno_11th_Editionr1.htm). [Official PDF](https://mutcd.fhwa.dot.gov/pdfs/11th_Editionr1/mutcd11theditionr1hl.pdf), Figure 4E-2 and Sections 4E.04–4E.05, printed pages 678–679: the simple vertical face orders red/yellow/green downward; the simple horizontal face orders them left to right as seen from the intended viewing direction. The official current PDF passages were inspected; this is not inferred from a stock icon.

The example does not establish other jurisdictions' rules, every permitted arrow/cluster configuration, housing color, pole dimensions or installation compliance. Find the applicable authority/equipment references. Do not apply road-signal rules to railway signals. Do not infer an animation sequence merely from the lens order.

Useful asset-specific checks: declared section count; color-role order in face-local coordinates; arrow direction; dimensions and mounting anchor. Visual checks: front/back orientation, lens/hood readability and plausible assembly. If animation is requested, test the selected documented state machine. Three colored circles and a clean GLB are insufficient evidence.
