Use case: stylized-concept. One original low-poly 3D concept for Coronacollina acula, specimen-informed Ediacaran fossil-form study. A small low thimble-shaped truncated cone with a gently flared wider base, three subtle raised rim nodes and a shallow central recess, NOT a deep tubular sponge opening. Four very long, thin, ruler-straight rigid needle-like spicules attach at four distinct points on the upper cone rim and radiate outward/downward in different directions without crossing each other. Their length is several times cone diameter, their thickness much less than cone radius; all four are visibly attached and clearly exposed. Four spicules are an illustrative reconstruction choice following studied articulated material, not a universal taxon claim. Matte ochre cone with slightly paler ivory-grey spicules, all closed faceted low-poly solids. Isolated whole subject on pale warm-grey studio background, three-quarter view high enough to show the shallow recess and all four ray directions. No rock slab, plinth, tentacles, soft waving rays, dense corona, extra organisms, text or watermark. Not an anemone and not a four-legged insect.
