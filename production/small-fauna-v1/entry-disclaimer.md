# Proposed entry notice

Draft only, for later museum integration. This recovery does not modify the museum.

## English

Welcome to an imaginative prehistoric world for young explorers. Our models draw on fossils and artistic imagination. Colors, textures, poses and some details are creative choices, and creatures from different times and places may appear together.

## 简体中文

欢迎来到为小朋友创造的史前想象世界。这里的模型参考化石资料，也加入了艺术想象。颜色、纹理、姿态和部分细节经过了创作，不同时代、不同地区的生物也可能在这里相遇。
