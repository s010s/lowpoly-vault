# Immutable original premodel design

Original genus-level fossil-specimen interpretation informed by the named Dickinsonia costata holotype SAM P47720, Evans et al2023 Fig1A. Not a scan, specimen facsimile or measured reconstruction. Source holotype is broadly oval with an undivided region and serial units. Ivantsov et al2019 argues the common ribbed mould represents decomposition-resistant support under an integument rather than complete exterior: this model is explicitly a preserved mould-form only, not whole living anatomy. Affinity, original tissues, fine symmetry and color remain uncertain. Nominal150x180x12mm slab, fossil about115x130mm illustrative, not measured SAM dimensions.
