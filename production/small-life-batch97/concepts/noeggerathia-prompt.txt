A single original low-poly scientific planning concept of one isolated sterile frond of the Carboniferous Noeggerathia foliosa, NOT the whole plant. Restrained ochre brown fossil-organ interpretation without a rock slab, on a plain pale neutral background. Based on the actual National Museum Prague holotype NM-E122a photograph, Sternbergiana 2021 Plate22 figure1, and Šimůnek & Bek 2003 primary description: slender single tapering rachis, approximately twelve subopposite pairs of broad obovate to obcuneate wedge-shaped pinnules, narrowed attached bases, broad rounded distal ends with shallow dentate or small lobed margins, radiating dichotomous fan venation not a leaf midrib. The first basal pairs are small and abortive, largest broad pinnules in the middle, progressively narrow small terminal pinnules. All leaflets genuinely attach to the rachis with slight upward inclination and slight basiscopic overlap, delicate thin volumetric geometry with flat facets. Show entire frond and short petiole, about 50cm illustrative total length, gently bowed, front three-quarter view. No cones, roots, trunk, flowers, fruits, branched fern subdivision, modern conifer needles, terminal spike or labels. This is artistic concept planning, not factual evidence; no source pixels copied.
