Use case: stylized-concept. Original low-poly 3D educational model concept of Dicranophyllum gallicum based on the unbranched reconstruction of Van Der Pas et al 2017. A very slender gently flexible upright single main stem with persistent narrow needle-like leaves in a clear spiral along most of its length, more dense and erect near tip. Each mature leaf is a thin solid blade which forks once and each arm forks again, four slender terminal tips per leaf, the second fork angle a little wider than first. Leaf bases turn upward from their attachment, no tufts on short shoots. Stem has small subtle elongated rhomboid leaf cushions, not huge pine bark. Narrow bottlebrush silhouette, basal stem modestly bare. Muted olive foliage, gray brown stem, flat-faceted opaque low-poly solid geometry, isolated warm off-white background and front three-quarter studio view. No pot, pedestal, roots, flowers, seeds, cones, multi-branched conifer crown, broad leaves or text. Whole habit and size are a conservative artistic reconstruction, not an exact preserved individual.
