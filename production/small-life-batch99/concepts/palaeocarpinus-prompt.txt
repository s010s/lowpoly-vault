Use case: scientific-educational stylized-concept. Original low-poly 3D concept for Palaeocarpinus joffrensis fossil infructescence, confined to the fruiting organ. Follow Sun and Stockey 1992 Figs1/2 S26016 and Figs4/6/11: slender short axis bearing six spaced cymules, each cymule holds two fruits on short pedicels. EACH fruit is a visible oval slightly flattened nutlet with faint longitudinal ridges, surrounded by TWO equal opposite deeply BILOBED bracts. Bract lobes have a few irregular pointed marginal teeth; the deep central cleft and two-lobe silhouette must be clear, not a generic pointed leaf. Arrange paired fruits so both nuts and supporting connections are exposed rather than hidden inside overlapping bracts. Warm dark chestnut nuts, muted tan thin solid bracts, brown axis. Full-volume flat-faceted low-poly matte shapes, no texture, pale warm background, elevated three-quarter view. No foliage, tree, flowers, pollen, staminate catkin, roots, rock matrix, wings detached in midair, or modern hornbeam single three-lobed leaf. Only the small specimen fruiting axis and attached paired fruit-bract units.
