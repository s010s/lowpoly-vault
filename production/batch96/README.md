# Batch96 original plant candidates

Paralycopodites, Lyginopteris, Medullosa and Cordaites. Maker candidates, not independent approval. GPU and composed-scene tests are not run. No publication permission follows from this folder.

Read `maker-handoff.json`, `handoff-assets.json` and `final-selection.json` for exact final revisions. Each has editable source, its actual generator/config/dependency closure, actual world-component/attachment measurements, source reopen, two distinct real queued rebuild receipts, project/Khronos/Three parse, eight fresh512px frames and a lossless contact sheet.

## Immutable design and uncertainty

The twelve original brief/reference/config files are pinned in `original-freeze-manifest.json`. Counts and technical ceilings are hard; dimensions are nominal artistic choices. Complete design and source scope were frozen before geometry. ImageGen images are design aids, never factual sources.

`reference-inspection-addendum.json` corrects grouped figure inspection wording: primary405/466 were inspected before freeze; supplemental404/467 first viewed later. `feature-source-map-correction-v002.json` supersedes an initially mechanically paired mapping without altering frozen files. Paralycopodites historical anatomy pixels do not prove whole architecture;2020 text supports its branch architecture, and inaccessible2020 original figures were not claimed viewed. Original specimen title and1980 synonymy retained. All whole-plant details and colors remain interpretations.

## Retained diagnostics and recovery

Initial Paralycopodites budget failure was repaired by lower subdivision, not a relaxed ceiling. R002 Medullosa exposed radial-frame reversal on its curved trunk; Medullosa and Cordaites were rebuilt with transported tube frames, remeasured and viewed from four diagnostic directions before final proof. No shader/camera workaround. Rejected versions and complete original media/process evidence remain local; see preservation inventory.

Native executor interruption stopped Cordaites first proof after five PNGs, without a complete manifest. That partial directory and original request/log remain local. Final Cordaites proof is a wholly new eight-frame run, not rebound old pixels.

Final selections retain their own pinned generators. The top-level generator is the latest repaired version and must not replace an earlier selection's generator for reproduction.
