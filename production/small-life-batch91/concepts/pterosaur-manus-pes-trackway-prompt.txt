Manus/pes lengths broadly comparable, each manus slightly outside and behind its associated pes, conservative illustrative sequence, no maker animal. Hero at a high angle, orthographic top and low edge view of the SAME slab; ivory background, natural low-poly facets, no texture decals, no text or labels. Three-pronged handprint and plain elongated foot shape must be unmistakable. Human-hand-looking five fingers, dinosaur three-toe pes, winged silhouette, raised tracks are prohibited.
