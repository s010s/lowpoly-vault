Use case: stylized-concept. Original low-poly 3D modeling concept sheet for a conservative Archaeopteris whole-tree reconstruction, choosing the relatively low, broad-paddle-leaved 3.5–4m hypothesis discussed by Snigirevsky et al. 2025 Fig9. Show one identical specimen in hero, front and foliage-detail panels on an ivory studio background. Woody tapering trunk, upward-spreading perennial branches making an upright vase-like crown, clear branch scars and modest flared roots. Flattened pinnate leafy sprays carry distinct broad rounded paddle/fan-like leaves on their rachises, not conifer needles or a modern fern rosette. Uneven natural azimuths and airy spaces between branch systems. Crown sits above a legible bare trunk; subdued olive green, sage, brown trunk. Entire specimen from root contact to top visible. Flat-faceted, full-volume shapes. No text, no fossils, no habitat scenery, no cones, flowers or fruits. This is planning artwork, not a preserved specimen claim. Do not reproduce any published illustration composition.
