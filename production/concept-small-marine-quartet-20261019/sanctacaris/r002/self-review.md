# Maker inspection — sanctacaris r002

This is production self-inspection, not an independent pass or publication decision.

Actual inspection: all twelve exact exported-GLB frames viewed on labeled contact sheets (320px per tile); critical concept-match and top revision pairs viewed at 512px per frame. Source concept pixels were inspected before modeling.

Swept triangular head wings are a clearly visible improvement in the top and oblique comparisons, replacing rectangular tabs. Six frontal limb pairs, eleven trunk plates, paired pale flaps and wide paddle tail remain readable.

Known differences and limits: Fine frontal dentition, podomere counts and setae are omitted; grasping tips are blunt. The frontal fan is more regular than the concept. Some stacked thin branches overlap from canonical front; top and underside resolve the pairs.

Volume: front, rear, both sides, top and bottom show shell depth and spatially separated limbs. Clay, alternate-key clay and wire views show the same real volume and authored topology. Parts are individually closed and intentionally overlap at attachments; this is a scene asset, not a printable fused solid. Tuzoia anterior attachment is specifically repaired in R003; R001/R002 are comparison history.

Acceptance remains pending: independent visual/factual/specification review, target browser/GPU/populated-scene QA, and the parent release process. Node GLTFLoader is only a geometry parser check. Gallery poster must be generated by the unified gallery renderer.
