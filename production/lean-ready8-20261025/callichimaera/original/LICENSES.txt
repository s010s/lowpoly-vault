Consulted scientific source:
Luque et al. (2019), Exceptional preservation of mid-Cretaceous marine arthropods and the
evolution of novel forms via heterochrony, Science Advances 5:eaav3875.
https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.aav3875
The paper states CC BY-NC 4.0; fossil photography and line drawings credited to Javier Luque,
3D scientific reconstruction to A. Duque. Consulted-only paper/figure files remain private and
are EXCLUDED from the deliverable manifest. No reference imagery, 3D model, scan, texture or
