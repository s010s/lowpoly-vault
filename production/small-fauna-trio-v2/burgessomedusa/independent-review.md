Decision: **pass_for_brief_with_advisories**. User acceptance: **pending**.

The revised asset visibly moves toward the requested natural, refined direction. Compared with the actually opened v001 source/browser hero and browser side, it replaces broad alternating lavender facets with a quiet seafoam shell, softer apex and shoulder transitions, a slightly contracted margin and a gentle swimming tilt. The short fringe remains subordinate to the bell. Source clay confirms the softer shape and shading survive color removal. This is a whole-revision comparison with changed geometry and pose, not a controlled material-only experiment.

The remaining art weakness is substantial enough to disclose: the tall smooth dome, regular rim and repeated fringe can still read as a lampshade or tasselled cover. In the exact browser GLB, transmission reveals four long internal ribs that sometimes reinforce that impression. The tilt helps the pose, but does not remove the ambiguity. It clears the brief’s basic-form recognition gate; that does not establish that the user will find it natural or attractive. No further cosmetic repair is requested in this bounded review.

All eight source previews, ten individual browser previews, and the two nine-instance scene views were opened at their actual paths. Browser transmission is materially more apparent than in the grainy, more opaque source renders, so use the browser hero when seeking the user’s visual decision. At 80 px the fingers merge into a narrow edge; 160–240 px preserve the bell/fringe hierarchy. No conspicuous detached major part, accidental penetration, broken shading, or unintended spike was seen. The lower cavity is open in browser bottom and source geometry, with four internal folds rather than a lower sealing cap.
