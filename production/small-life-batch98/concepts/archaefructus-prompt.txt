Use case: stylized-concept. Original low-poly 3D living reconstruction concept of Archaefructus sinensis, following the organ organization of Sun et al. 2002 Science Fig2 A-D,H,I and Fig3 but not copying their drawing. An airy hand-scale aquatic herb with a slender green central stem dividing into four thin ascending shoots. Finely three-times divided fanlike leaves along the lower stems, longer petioles on basal leaves, swollen petiole bases. Four open elongated terminal reproductive axes: small paired stamens below stalked narrow folded carpels, with clear empty spaces, no perianth at all. A few simple sparse rootlets at the base. No flowers with petals, sepals, floral cups, rosettes, lotus discs or fern croziers. No water, terrain, pot, pedestal, labels. Restrained green, olive and muted ochre reproductive units, full-volume opaque faceted low-poly geometry. Entire plant seen on warm neutral studio backdrop, isolated three-quarter orthographic hero. This is explicitly an uncertain aquatic-habit reconstruction, not the proven first flower or a precise fossil cast.
