Use case: stylized-concept. Create an original low-poly 3D production concept of a hand-scale Sciadopitys cf. marcodurensis fossil branch specimen, not a whole tree and not a pine. Based on inspected Palamarev et al. 1991 Plate 4 figure 9: a short gently bent woody axis bears three separated whorls of 12–15 very long linear entire blades, each roughly 8–10 cm long and 2 mm wide, rounded narrowed tips and a shallow longitudinal groove. Whorls fan out like umbrella spokes, not pine-needle fascicles, with clear empty intervals of stem between. Preserve the radial organization in modest three-dimensional relief rather than a flat decal. Muted charcoal-brown stem and warm umber fossil blades, opaque faceted solids. Elegant calm museum asset, isolated on a warm neutral background, fully visible orthographic three-quarter hero view. Show no rock slab, labels, pedestal, roots, cones, flowers, extra trees, plastic shine, painterly texture or microscopic detail. This is interpretive concept art, not a photograph or scientific proof.
