# Immutable original premodel design

Generic original flat-top tuya geological form with features supported by primary subglacial-volcanic descriptions and actual source pixels. Not an exact Togiak,TuyaButte,Okmok reconstruction, DEM, age, internal stratigraphic section or eruption simulation. Nominal1200x950x350m module illustrative, not site measurement. Broad basaltic subaerial cap above water/ice-contact tuff/breccia; localized pillow-fragment-like relief is stylized external exposure, not assertion a universal stratum is exposed around every tuya.

- Broad near-flat coherent lava cap with steep irregular ice-confined volcanic flanks
- Dark jointed cap over subdued fragmental tuff/breccia flank; localized rounded pillow-fragment-like integral relief and shallow asymmetric notch
- Not sedimentary mesa recolor, stacked disconnected disks or cratered conical volcano

Keep concept broad flat dark cap and steep flanks, but break perfect circular plateau into irregular elongated polygonal footprint. Avoid decorative cap mosaic and oversized identical columns. A continuous closed volume with integral cap joints and lower flank relief, not intersecting stone disks or loose boulder pile. One modest flank notch, low asymmetric talus. Cap and volcanic fragmental flank geometry have real geological rationale, not only material recolor of mesa. No summit crater or modern snow/forest. Actual EGU photo shows broad flat cap plus cirques; exact cirque count not copied. AlaskaFig5 shows eroded remnant and breccia, not intact flat-top edifice.
