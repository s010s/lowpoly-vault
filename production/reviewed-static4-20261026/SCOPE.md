This scope document does not certify their execution.
- Existing reports of unknown, pending or failed GPU, scene, physics, user, deployment, or full-formal checks are not changed by source compatibility or bounded static review. No new universal eight-view, physical-GPU, scene, collision, rigging or world-placement acceptance is asserted.

## Scientific interpretation

Every model retains its bilingual scientific limits and original reference attribution. References are consulted sources, not incorporated reference pixels.

Nipponites is shell-only; Paradoxides is a dorsal exoskeleton with a technical closing underside, not reconstructed ventral anatomy; Yochelcionella is an MGUH 27016-informed solid internal-mould art study at a nominal one-millimetre height, not a hollow living shell. These three are fossil-specimen studies rather than complete living organisms. Origin-only corrections for Camptosaurus, Diictodon and Thrinaxodon inherit only the explicitly documented prior morphology review; Mammuthus and Dapedium preserve their original brief-revision inheritance. Mammuthus uses the documented mean of four sole centres. Habelia preserves the species-level twelve-segment interpretation and the conflicting broader family wording. Proganochelys retains the bounded reviewed contacts without declaring physics or rig readiness.
