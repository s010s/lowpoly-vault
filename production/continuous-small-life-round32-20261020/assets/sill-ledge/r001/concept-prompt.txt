Original low-poly geological teaching outcrop. Thick resistant dark gray igneous sill sheet lies parallel to gently dipping tan sedimentary host bedding, exposed physical projecting ledge. Pale narrow baked contact margins immediately above and below intrusion. Host rock several subordinate bed planes, irregular erosion. The intrusive body has substantial actual thickness, a projecting ledge and broken blocky joint faces. Not a crosscutting vertical dike, coal stripe, recolor or lava flow. Three-quarter, front and side on neutral white. Geometry-focused facets, no text.
