## Rangea
Original inventory scope is fossil specimen, with several ribbed vanes converging into a three-dimensional spindle-like form, not a confidently living plant or modern sea pen. The six radial bilaminar vanes and hierarchy must be distinguishable from the earlier single-plane frond models, not a recolor or rotated duplicate.

## Serac
Official source https://www.nps.gov/articles/seracsandicebergs.htm contains an actual Emmons Glacier sunrise photograph, NPS/Ryan Volum. Choose serac pixels, not its separate floating-iceberg image. Original form is an angular ice tower isolated by intersecting deep crevasses; glacier context and crevasse boundaries must distinguish it from a generic pointed rock or batch75 glacial horn. No transparent-material trick to substitute for physical ice-block geometry. No exact glacier, real-world dimensions or hazard simulation claim.
