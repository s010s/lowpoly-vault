# Immutable premodel brief

Generic original geological serac form informed by modern glacier analogues, no named glacier, age, exact dimensions or hazard simulation.

- One dominant angular ice tower
- Two intersecting deep physical crevasses isolate tower from lower glacier
- Unequal blunt shoulders and faceted ice walls
- Continuous supporting glacier tile, not floating iceberg/mountain

Concept shows correct intersecting crevasses and raised block, but too regular cross-shaped channels and sugary fractured texture. Build irregular angular polygonal tower with tilted blunt crown, four lower surrounding ice fields and two unequal crossing channels reaching down through the tile relief. All opaque palette-only geometry, no floating blocks or hidden overlaps.

Personally read NPS serac text and inspected actual NPS/Ryan Volum Emmons Glacier sunrise photograph. It shows angular broken serrated ice towers/ridges, steep fractured walls and irregular glacier surface, not floating ocean icebergs; separate iceberg photograph not used.
