## Batch72

### baiera
- Original scope is a fossil leaf specimen, not a reconstructed whole tree. Use one documented outline with a few branching flat wedges, deep cuts and genuine open gaps, not a broad Ginkgo fan or a spray of cylindrical needles. Do not silently transfer a Sphenobaiera identification.
- Original museum source was actually opened in coordinator preflight: https://shmuseum.hku.hk/education/earth-evolution/mesozoic/dinosaur-world/plants-in-the-dinosaur-world
- Its Baiera asadai image link was resolved to https://shmuseum.hku.hk/f/page/1046/528p297/1.%20CH106.JPG . The coordinator has not yet personally inspected those image pixels. The museum labels it Upper Jurassic/Jehol/Liaoning and 11.5cm, but this catalogue combination should not be uncritically converted into an exact locality/age assertion. Resolve taxonomy and suitable original selected specimen before freeze.
- Additional primary leads, not yet personally inspected here: https://nph.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/nph.20154 (2025 study of Late Triassic Baiera multipartita, figure1b specimen YNUPB10185), https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3622679/ (2013 comparative foliage figures), and https://data.jncc.gov.uk/data/d249bbc2-220e-4e39-afa6-1019ee91917e/gcr-v22-mesozoic-tertiary-palaeobotany-c3.pdf . Do not mix species outlines or imply Triassic reference specimens are Jurassic. A bounded genus-level original specimen interpretation may cite comparison sources, with era/source disagreement stated accurately.

### transverse-dune-ridge
- Original gate explicitly requires one continuous asymmetric slipface and a longer gentle stoss ramp. Do not rotate the current linear dune, duplicate barchans or produce a symmetrical ridge.
- Actual primary text opened: Nicholas Lancaster 2009, https://www.nps.gov/articles/aeolian.htm . Figure4 distinguishes dune types; Figure5 labels and narrative differ in panel references, so inspect the actual image labels, not just nearby prose.
- Genuine original geometry must establish an asymmetric cross-section and laterally continuous ridge in neutral clay as well as beauty views. Scientific process analogue, not a claimed measured site. The NPS-hosted Lancaster article/figures are copyrighted by GSA; consult only and preserve rights notes.

### braided-river-reach
- Original form is multiple shallow channels repeatedly splitting and rejoining around exposed bars, not one meandering channel with decorative islets.
- Actual NPS page opened: https://home.nps.gov/grte/learn/nature/snake-river-rem.htm . Inspect the actual NPS Potter aerial photograph and distinguish it from the elevation visualization. Additional official context: https://www.nps.gov/subjects/geology/fluvial-landforms.htm .
- Coherent physical floodplain, inset channels and exposed bars; avoid perfectly repeating channel widths/oval islands. Static generic reach, no flow simulation or exact Snake River replica claim.

### geode-cavity
- Original gate requires a free rounded nodule, enclosing rind, hollow topology and inward crystal lining. This must differ geometrically from a crystal pocket in continuous bedrock and the earlier dogtooth-spar cavity. Freeze any deliberate broken/cut display opening honestly before modeling, retaining most of the enclosing nodule.
- Actual Utah Geological Survey page opened: https://geology.utah.gov/popular/rocks-minerals/rock-mineral-collecting-sites/dugway-geodes/ . Inspect actual specimen images. Additional geological survey lead: https://iowageologicalsurvey.uiowa.edu/iowa-geology/popular-interest/iowa-geodes .
- The source inventory also includes an irrelevant Fort Matanzas coquina source. Preserve that inventory unchanged but explicitly flag non-support and build a corrected real geode reference ledger before freeze. Do not pretend coquina supports geodes.

## Batch73

### fractofusus
- Original scope is a fossil-form specimen study, not a confident whole-organism reconstruction or modern leaf. Ground-hugging fusiform organization with opposing hierarchical branches; no upright stem, root, mouth or invented stolons.
- Actual 2024 primary text opened: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11214322/ , DOI10.1016/j.isci.2024.110107. Its Figure2 contains actual Fractofusus misrai fossils; a separate reconstruction used for CFD is not itself the fossil. Personally inspect the actual specimens and retain interpretation uncertainty. Article is CC BY-NC-ND: consult only, do not incorporate published reconstructions/meshes/images.
- Additional primary leads: https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/geological-magazine/article/morphological-variation-in-the-rangeomorph-organism-fractofusus-misrai-from-the-ediacaran-of-newfoundland-canada/7B405D3A13987524FE3B7DFCDEA37854 (2022, fusiform misrai versus ovoid andersoni), https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-025-58605-9 (2025 andersoni, do not transfer blindly), https://www.nature.com/articles/nature14646 (2015 actual specimens and population interpretation).

### striated-bedrock
- Original target is long aligned grooves physically incised through a smoothed bedrock surface. Not painted stripes or a second roche-moutonnee knob.
- Official source lead: https://www.nps.gov/romo/learn/education/upload/Geology-Teacher-Guide-for-web.pdf . Read the relevant section and inspect real groove photographs, not unrelated erratic imagery. Establish natural unequal groove depth/width and readable relief in clay.

### fumarole-vent
- Original target is an irregular fractured opening and mineral-encrusted rim. Not another mudpot basin, raised round geyser cone or steam effect with no diagnostic geometry.
- Actual NPS page opened: https://home.nps.gov/yell/learn/nature/hydrothermal-features.htm . Inspect the actual fumarole reference, not adjacent geyser/mudpot pictures. Generic static vent; do not claim gas chemistry, operating temperature or dynamic flow was measured.

### banded-iron-formation
- Original gate requires fine persistent iron-rich/silica-rich laminae with real alternating erosion ribs, not a recolored generic cliff. Formation age is distinct from later exposure age.
- Actual museum page opened: https://australian.museum/learn/minerals/mineral-factsheets/banded-iron-formation/ . Inspect actual DR.19296 specimen photograph. The page has a suspicious specimen-dimension string; do not copy it into measured asset dimensions. Consult-only image.
- Additional primary/official leads: https://www.amnh.org/exhibitions/permanent/planet-earth/how-has-the-earth-evolved/banded-iron-formation and https://pubs.usgs.gov/of/2005/1294/a/of2005-1294a.pdf . Avoid an overconfident claim that every band is annual or a single mechanism is conclusively established.
