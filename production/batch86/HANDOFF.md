## Known limits

- Plant capsules, their notches and short stalks are small at512px; the reconstructed branch/root/capsule population is deliberately reduced, not a fossil census. Root caps/hairs are not asserted. Geometry is editable components, not a printable Boolean union.
- Beach cardinal views are genuinely edge-on and less informative than the elevated views; low relief is still present in clay. Dimensions and repeated pattern are illustrative, not a DEM or swash simulation.
- Cinder-cone oxidized color sector and erosion detail are deliberately simplified; there is no eruption-state inference.
- Tower-karst is a generic low-poly four-tower group, not a named-site reconstruction. Adjacent closed plain quadrants share interior boundary planes, not overlapping exposed top overlays. Some small towers are naturally occluded at level views but visible from top/reversehero.
- Provider internal generation timestamps and precise generator-authoring start time were not supplied or measured. Actual observed invocation/result boundaries and freeze-before-authoring attestation remain. The supplemental concept integrity checker is valid but evidence_complete=false for those unavailable exact times; no invented metadata.
