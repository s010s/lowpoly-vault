# Immutable premodel brief

Generic original human-scale coal seam outcrop form, not a named mine, age, coal rank, gas/permeability measurement or copied existing stratified cliff.

- Recessed three-dimensional coal seam
- Projecting resistant host beds above and below
- Long uneven face cleats
- Shorter near-orthogonal butt cleats terminate against face cleats
- Irregular spaced cleated blocks, not decorative grid

Coal recess is real depth behind two host volumes. Unequal x/depth cleat block outlines, staggered terminating cross cuts, irregular chipped fronts; do not turn bedding-plane butt cleats into uniform horizontal brick courses. Concept has too many masonry-like horizontal coal splits; remove regular horizontal grid in geometry. No copied source pixels or regular decorative blocks.
