Pleurotomaria r001 reproducibility

Requires Blender 4.3.2 (bundled Python, bpy, bmesh and mathutils). Generator helpers are bundled beside this file. No mesh, image texture, network download or another asset directory is required.

From the delivery directory:
blender -b -t 2 --factory-startup --python-exit-code 1 --python source/generate.py -- --output /tmp/pleurotomaria-rebuild

This produces model.blend, model.glb and build-report.json in the selected output. Do not point a rebuild at the fixed reviewed candidate. A different output directory preserves its identity and hashes.

Exact-export four-view evidence can be regenerated to an empty output folder:
blender -b -t 2 --factory-startup --python-exit-code 1 --python source/deps/render_views.py -- model.glb --output-dir /tmp/pleurotomaria-views --revision r001 --brief brief.json --references references.json --resolution 768 --samples 48 --turntable-frames 0 --views front,side,top,perspective

The last command imports the exact GLB. It does not establish independent visual acceptance or actual WebGL GPU correctness. Render helpers are canonical fixed snapshots plus a small four-view camera adapter. Their exact hashes and runtime versions are in source/dependencies.json.

Geometry is original and intended as an interpretive fossil shell, not a print-ready exact specimen scan. The inferred slit and missing lip are disclosed in brief.json, references.json and the factual-boundaries evidence.
