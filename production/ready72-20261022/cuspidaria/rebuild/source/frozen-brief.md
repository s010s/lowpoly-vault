# Cuspidaria original #910, single-valve exterior candidate r001

Frozen 2026-10-22, after inspecting the real S1 photograph and the one genuine ImageGen concept, before geometry construction. Evidence-informed fossil-form study, not a complete specimen reconstruction. Anchor: Pliocene Cuspidaria depontaillieri holotype MNHN.F.J04629. Original inventory identity is retained.

The generated concept is a palette/faceting guide only. Its body is too low and its rostrum too long compared with the named fossil: correct to a rounded body approximately two-thirds of total length and a broad overall height approximately 0.60 of length. A raised asymmetric upper umbonal shoulder, inflated exterior and a distinctly slender, blunt, continuous posterior rostrum are the three primary obligations. The transition is asymmetrical, more abrupt along the lower shoulder. Use an original designed profile, not pixel tracing. No radial ribs, spines, horn, siphon, second valve or soft anatomy.

Model one continuous closed technical solid. Subtle concentric growth relief belongs to the exterior surface itself, with no floating decorative bands. Quiet, simply closed back: unobserved thickness/back geometry is an artistic export completion, not claimed internal shell anatomy. Omit teeth and a false hinge interior. Depth is artistic, bounded around 1.5 mm. Nominal projected length 8 mm is approximate from S1's image bar, never a museum measurement. Do not use S2 dimensions or species-specific rib count.

Blender meters, X long axis (rostrum -X), Z up, exterior -Y, glTF Y up and exterior +Z. Root identity at upright display lower edge center. No physical posture claim. Palette-only ivory limestone, two materials maximum with back differentiated subtly. Intentionally faceted surface, no image textures. Budget <=6000 exported triangles, <=2 primitives, <=2 materials.
