A modest young spreading tree with exposed brown woody branching framework, airy open crown and individually readable leaves. About24 green leaves, each single connected palmate blade with SEVEN very narrow long fingerlike pointed lobes separated by deep sinuses; basal lobe angles about20degrees, central lobe longest and outer lobes progressively shorter, almost entire margins. NOT separate leaflets, broad maple leaves, fan leaves or cannabis serrations. Three hanging slender axes outside the crown bear tiny ochre globose fruit heads, several along each axis, explicitly small compared with15–35cm leaves; no oversized orange fruit. Overall artistic tree about2m tall, leaves about0.22m long; inferred habit not intact fossil. Show main three-quarter tree, a small closeup leaf and reproductive axis.
